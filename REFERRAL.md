# Referral message templates

Job Radar adds a "your connections at <company>" link to alerts for top employers. It opens LinkedIn's own people
search in YOUR browser. You read the profiles and send the message yourself, which is safe for your account.

Rules that make referrals work:
- Message 1 to 3 people per company, not 20. Pick people on the relevant team, or from your college.
- Ask for advice first; a referral often follows. Keep it short.
- Put the job link in the message. Attach your resume only if they ask.
- Follow up once after 5 to 7 days. Thank them either way.

## 1. First-degree connection (you know them a little)
Hi [Name], hope you are doing well! I am a 3rd-year B.Tech CS student and I just saw the [Role] internship at
[Company]: [job link]. My background is in [your strongest project/skill, one line]. Would you be open to referring
me, or sharing any advice on applying? Happy to send my resume. Thank you!

## 2. Second-degree (a mutual connection)
Hi [Name], I noticed we are both connected to [Mutual name]. I am a 3rd-year B.Tech CS student interested in the
[Role] internship at [Company]: [job link]. Could I ask you two quick questions about the team and the interview?
Thank you for your time.

## 3. Alumni or same community (cold message, under 300 characters for the connection note)
Hi [Name], I am a 3rd-year CS student from [your college/community]. I am applying for the [Role] internship at
[Company]. Would love your advice on the process if you have a minute. Thank you!

## 4. Follow-up after a week
Hi [Name], a quick follow-up on my earlier note about the [Role] internship at [Company] ([job link]). No worries if
you are busy. Thank you again for considering it!
